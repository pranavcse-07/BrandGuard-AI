# 🛡️ BrandGuard AI
### AI-Powered Brand Governance & Content Compliance Platform

**Every message represents your brand. Make sure it represents it correctly.**

BrandGuard AI is an intelligent brand-governance platform designed to help organisations maintain a consistent, trustworthy and professional brand identity across all their content.

It reviews content before publication by comparing it against approved brand guidelines, facts, terminology and visual assets. It identifies inconsistencies, explains why they matter, provides evidence-backed correction suggestions and enables human reviewers to approve or reject content.

Rather than simply rejecting content, BrandGuard AI helps creators improve their work while preserving their original message and creative intent.

---

## 📌 1. The Problem

Organisations publish content through multiple employees, marketing teams, agencies and AI-powered tools.

As a result, maintaining a consistent brand identity becomes difficult.

Common problems include:

- Incorrect or outdated product information.
- Inconsistent brand names and terminology.
- Content that does not match the company's tone.
- Unsupported promotional and marketing claims.
- Incorrect logos, colours and visual assets.
- Accessibility issues in digital content.
- Lack of a proper content review and approval process.

These mistakes can damage an organisation's credibility and reputation.

**Our solution:** A centralised AI-powered platform that checks content against approved brand standards before it gets published.

---

## 💡 2. Our Solution

BrandGuard AI acts as a digital brand-quality control system.

It provides a unified environment where organisations can:

1. Define and manage their brand guidelines.
2. Upload content for compliance checking.
3. Detect factual, linguistic and visual inconsistencies.
4. Understand why each issue was identified.
5. Receive intelligent correction suggestions.
6. Collaborate through a human review and approval workflow.
7. Track recurring mistakes and improve brand governance.

Our approach combines rule-based validation, AI-powered analysis and human decision-making.

---

## ⭐ 3. Key Features

### 1. Brand Guidelines Management
- Upload and manage brand guideline documents.
- Define approved brand names, terminology and phrases.
- Configure brand tone and communication standards.
- Store approved product information and promotional facts.
- Maintain official logos, colours and visual assets.

### 2. AI-Powered Content Checker
- Analyse uploaded text and documents.
- Detect inconsistent terminology and brand messaging.
- Identify tone and language inconsistencies.
- Detect unsupported promotional claims.
- Identify incorrect or outdated information.
- Check accessibility-related issues.

### 3. Evidence-Based Issue Detection

Every detected issue includes:
- Highlighted problematic content.
- Violation category and severity.
- Explanation of the problem.
- Relevant approved brand guideline or source.
- Suggested correction.

### 4. Claim Verification Meter

Evaluates promotional claims against approved company information.

Claims are classified as:
- **Verified:** Supporting evidence is available.
- **Unverified:** Sufficient evidence is not available.
- **Contradicted:** The claim conflicts with an approved source.

### 5. Meaning Preservation Score

AI correction suggestions should preserve the creator's original purpose.

The system compares the original and corrected content to identify whether the suggested revision changes the intended meaning or introduces new unsupported claims.

### 6. Visual Brand Compliance
- Detect incorrect or unauthorised logos.
- Compare colours against approved brand colours.
- Identify logo distortion and placement issues.
- Extract image text using OCR.
- Identify text readability and accessibility issues.

### 7. Human Review and Approval
- Creator submits content for review.
- Manager reviews detected issues and suggestions.
- Authorised reviewers approve or request revisions.
- Users can add comments and track changes.
- Maintain revision and approval history.

### 8. Brand Governance Analytics
- Track frequently occurring violations.
- Identify repeated content mistakes.
- Monitor compliance trends.
- Detect unclear or conflicting brand guidelines.
- Generate governance reports.

## 🔐 10. Privacy, Security & Responsible AI

BrandGuard AI is designed with responsible AI principles.

- Use authentication and role-based access.
- Protect confidential brand documents.
- Keep API keys in environment variables.
- Never expose private API credentials in frontend code.
- Restrict access to uploaded content.
- Use approved organisational sources for factual verification.
- Clearly distinguish unverified claims from false claims.
- Provide explanations and evidence for detected issues.
- Keep humans responsible for final approvals.
- Disclose external AI providers and data-processing practices.
